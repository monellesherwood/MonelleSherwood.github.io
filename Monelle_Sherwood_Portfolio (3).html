Featured Venture

Nature Glow

Nature Glow creates organic, customizable bar soaps infused with authentic Jamaican essential oils, giving eco-conscious and sensitive-skin consumers an affordable luxury that is organic, deeply personal, and rooted in Caribbean heritage.

The Problem

Most commercially available skincare products are loaded with synthetic chemicals, artificial fragrances, and harsh additives that irritate sensitive skin and contribute to environmental harm. Despite a growing consumer demand for organic and personalized personal care products, very few brands combine true ingredient transparency with meaningful cultural authenticity. The $7.57 billion global organic soap market remains largely dominated by mass producers who offer little to no customization, leaving eco-conscious buyers — especially those who value both sustainability and heritage — deeply underserved. Locally in Columbia, SC, no competitor currently bridges the gap between organic formulation, hands-on customization, and Caribbean cultural identity at an affordable price.

Our Solution

Nature Glow produces premium, natural bar soaps using a high-efficiency melt-and-pour technique with organic shea butter bases and genuine Jamaican essential oils — including Pimento, Castor, Lemongrass, and Peppermint — sourced directly from a 40-year-old family-owned farm in Jamaica. Customers can fully customize their soap's fragrance, oil blend, and eco-friendly packaging, creating a product that is uniquely their own. The customer journey begins with discovery via Instagram, TikTok, or Facebook, then moves to purchase through the Shopify storefront or in person at Columbia pop-up markets like Soda City. Every product is packaged in 100% biodegradable, recyclable materials, reinforcing the brand's commitment to sustainability from farm to shower.

Target Market

Nature Glow's ideal customer is an eco-conscious individual aged 13–60 living in the Columbia, SC metropolitan area — middle-income households willing to pay a modest premium for clean, natural skincare that aligns with their values. The global organic soap industry represents a Total Addressable Market (TAM) of $7.57 billion, while Nature Glow's Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM) narrows to $6.86 million within South Carolina's artisanal segment. In Year 1, Nature Glow targets a Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM) of $26,790, growing to $40,185 by Year 3 as the brand expands its digital presence and repeat customer base.

Revenue Model

Nature Glow generates income through a direct-to-consumer model across three channels: online sales via Shopify and social commerce platforms (Instagram, TikTok Shop, Facebook Marketplace), and in-person sales at local Columbia pop-up markets. The business offers three product lines — Small Bars at $10, Medium Bars at $14, and Bundle Gift Sets at $30 — yielding an average selling price of $15.23 and a blended contribution margin of 73.8%. Additional revenue streams include customization add-ons and a digital loyalty program designed to increase repeat purchases, with a target of 50+ returning customers in Year 1.

Competitive Advantage

Nature Glow's primary moat is the exclusive combination of authentic Jamaican essential oil sourcing and deep customer customization — a pairing no competitor in the Columbia, SC market currently offers. The direct relationship with a 40-year-old family-owned farm in Jamaica creates a supply chain barrier that competitors cannot easily replicate. The brand's "Affordable Luxury" positioning at $10–$30 captures value-driven consumers who want more than mass market but cannot access premium boutique pricing. Planned trademark protection for the Nature Glow name, logo, and signature packaging will further deepen intellectual property barriers.

Growth & Vision

Capstone Reflection

My transformation from student to professional, in my own words.

01
My Journey at Benedict
My time at Benedict College and in BA 432 taught me that entrepreneurship is far more than having a great idea; it demands resilience, discipline, and an unwavering commitment to the details that most people overlook. Building Nature Glow from concept to a fully developed business plan showed me that the entrepreneurial journey is not linear. There were moments where I had to pause, reassess, and pivot, and those moments taught me more than any smooth stretch of progress ever could. I learned that even the smallest miscalculation — a single financial figure entered incorrectly — can create a ripple effect that requires you to trace every number back to the beginning. That level of accountability pushed me to become more meticulous and intentional in everything I produce. Beyond the technical skills, I also learned a great deal about myself. I rise to the challenge, take complex ideas and communicate them clearly, and genuinely care about building something meaningful. Senior Defense Day was a defining moment in that growth. Standing in front of a panel of judges and pitching my business forced me to own my work with confidence. I had to articulate not just what Nature Glow was, but why it mattered — and doing that successfully showed me that I am more capable than I sometimes give myself credit for.
02
Challenges & Breakthroughs
One of the most significant challenges I faced during my capstone was ensuring the accuracy and integrity of my financial projections. Every figure had to be grounded in real industry data, sourced from credible references, and internally consistent across every financial statement. The pressure of that was immense because a single error — an incorrect cost per unit, a mislinked formula, or an unsupported assumption — would throw off the entire model and require me to trace the problem all the way back to its origin. I overcame this challenge by building my spreadsheet with linked cells so that any change would automatically update throughout the model, and by documenting every source as I researched, so nothing was left unverifiable. Beyond the financials, I also had to make strategic pivots to the business itself, including expanding the product line to strengthen the revenue model and improve overall feasibility. These adjustments were not setbacks; they were breakthroughs in disguise because they forced me to think like a real business owner responding to real market conditions. If I could do anything differently, I would establish a source-documentation habit from day one — recording where every figure comes from before moving forward — rather than trying to reconstruct that trail later.
03
My Professional Future
In five years, I see myself working as a market research analyst, ideally after completing a master's degree in business analytics. My capstone experience in BA 432 was instrumental in building that foundation. Conducting industry research, sizing a market using TAM, SAM, and SOM frameworks, analyzing competitors, and translating raw data into a coherent business strategy are all skills I now carry with genuine confidence. Nature Glow was not just a class project; it was proof that I can take an idea, research it rigorously, and build a credible, data-backed plan around it. The skills I will carry forward most are market research and analysis, financial literacy, public speaking, and the ability to communicate complex information clearly to different audiences. Senior Defense Day in particular sharpened my ability to present under pressure and think critically on my feet — a skill that will serve me in any professional environment. As a leader, I do not aspire to lead through authority or rigid hierarchy. I want to be a transformational leader — someone who earns trust by being reliable, who sets a clear vision and empowers others to execute it, and who creates an environment where people feel motivated to do their best work not because they have to, but because they want to.
